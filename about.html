About

Exit Node is a studio and press based in Prague. It surfaces material from the internet's closed channels into identities, objects, film, and the web.

We work with artists and brands across the whole arc of a project: identity and art direction, product and design, commerce and web, 3D, film and sound.

Some of the work is building something from nothing. Some is repairing what already runs.

Engagements range from a single commission to running the operation outright.
